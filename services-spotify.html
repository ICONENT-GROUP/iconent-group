The Method

How we run it

Three phases. Same structure every time. The work inside each phase is custom to the catalog.

Step 01 — Catalog Audit

We benchmark every track against the signals Spotify actually rewards — save rate, completion, and skip behavior — and flag which ones are pitch-ready vs. which need to be re-cut or repositioned before they hit a curator's desk. We also map your sound against comparable artists currently breaking on Release Radar and Discover Weekly.

Step 02 — Release Sequencing

Release sequencing and creative direction tuned to platform behavior. We plan singles on the cycles that match your audience's listening curve, design covers that hold up at thumbnail size, and structure track intros for retention past the first 30 seconds — the window the algorithm watches before it decides whether to push you further.

Step 03 — Editorial & Ads

Editorial pitching, paid amplification, and post-release retention. We pitch through verified editor relationships and the official Spotify for Artists tool, run Spotify and Meta ad campaigns with real landing pages, and monitor first-72-hour save rate to push the algorithm in your favor before momentum dies.
