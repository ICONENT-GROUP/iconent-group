The Method

How we run it

Three phases. Same structure every time. The work inside each phase is custom to the catalog.

Step 01 — Catalog Audit

We identify which tracks have the right hook structure, BPM, and emotional register to actually move on the platform — and which ones need to be re-cut as 15-second or 30-second sound clips before they go out. We also map creator categories that match your genre and audience demo.

Step 02 — Creator Network

Creator briefing and sound packaging across both micro and macro influencers. We design briefs that build trends around the cut of your track — not generic challenges, but creative formats coherent with the audio of your release. Spark Ad and in-feed creative direction tied to the seeded content so paid and organic reinforce each other.

Step 03 — Seeding & Ads

Seeding, paid ads, and amplification with growth consulting. We roll out the creator network in waves to keep the sound live longer, run TikTok ads on the seeded content, and track the metrics that actually correlate with career growth — sound usage, save rate, and downstream Spotify behavior, not just views.
