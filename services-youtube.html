The Method

How we run it

Three phases. Same structure every time. The work inside each phase is custom to the channel.

Step 01: Channel Audit

We diagnose the structural issues most artists never fix: bad metadata, missing end screens, dead playlists, and videos that get clicks but no watch time. We map which catalog tracks are ready for proper music videos vs. which should drop as Shorts first to seed the channel.

Step 02: Video & Shorts

Music video and Shorts creative direction. We brief videographers around hooks that survive the first 3 seconds, structure visuals for view-through rate (not just views), and design Shorts that pull viewers back to the main channel, turning passive impressions into subscribers, saves, and downstream Spotify plays.

Step 03: TrueView Campaigns

Paid YouTube ad campaigns optimized for view rate and cost per qualified view, with audience targeting built on how people listen, instead of lookalikes that burn budget. Monthly reporting covers view rate, completion, CPV, and downstream platform conversion.
