The Method

How it fits

YouTube Promotion runs inside the broader 3-step framework.

Step 01 — Foundation

Channel and catalog audit for YouTube fit. We diagnose the structural issues most artists never fix — bad metadata, missing end screens, dead playlists, and videos that get clicks but no watch time. We map which catalog tracks are ready for proper music videos vs. which should drop as Shorts first to seed the channel.

Step 02 — Sound & Brand

Music video and Shorts creative direction. We brief videographers around hooks that survive the first 3 seconds, structure visuals for view-through rate (not just views), and design Shorts that actually pull viewers back to the main channel — turning passive impressions into subscribers, saves, and downstream Spotify plays.

Step 03 — Launch

TrueView campaigns and channel growth execution. We run paid YouTube ad campaigns optimized for view rate and cost per qualified view, with audience targeting built on real listener behavior — not lookalikes that burn budget. Monthly reporting covers view rate, completion, CPV, and downstream platform conversion.
