The Method

How we run it

Three phases. Same structure every time. The work inside each phase is custom to the profile.

Step 01: Profile Audit

We diagnose the bottlenecks most artists never see: feed cohesion, bio funnel, Story drop-off, Reels retention curves. We benchmark you against artists in your genre who are converting at scale, then identify exactly which formats are working for them and which are dead weight on your profile.

Step 02: Reels & Creative

Reels and ads creative direction, plus editorial placements. We brief hooks engineered to survive the first 1.5 seconds, select the cut from your track that lands hardest, and coordinate placements with relevant pages. Ad creative is made to move viewers from impression to landing page instead of a dead profile view.

Step 03: Paid Funnel

Daily execution, paid funnel, and organic growth consulting. We run a consistent posting cadence, Meta ad campaigns with full-funnel tracking, engagement strategy, and DM-funnel for warm leads. Monthly reporting on reach, save rate, follower quality, and the metrics labels look at when they sign artists.
