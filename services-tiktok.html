The Method

How it fits

TikTok Promotion runs inside the broader 3-step framework.

Step 01 — Foundation

Catalog audit for TikTok fit and sound selection. We identify which tracks have the right hook structure, BPM, and emotional register to actually move on the platform — and which ones need to be re-cut as 15-second or 30-second sound clips before they go out. We also map creator categories that match your genre and audience demo.

Step 02 — Sound & Brand

Creator briefing and sound packaging, with personalized influencer strategy. We design briefs that get creators to use the sound in ways that actually drive discovery — not one-off paid posts that die in 24 hours. Spark Ad and in-feed creative direction tied to the seeded content so paid and organic reinforce each other.

Step 03 — Launch

Seeding, paid ads, and amplification with growth consulting. We roll out the creator network in waves to keep the sound live longer, run TikTok ads on the seeded content, and track the metrics that actually correlate with career growth — sound usage, save rate, and downstream Spotify behavior, not just views.
